# Divine Days — World

## Mortal realms

1. Shireville
2. The Enchanted Forest
3. The Underworld
4. The Nexus of Planes

Every realm has four class-aligned locations: Paladin/armor, Barbarian/weapons, Rogue/skills, and Mage/spells. Shireville uses the Barracks, Camp, Tavern, and Tower. Later locations retain the same mechanical roles with unique cultures, enemies, and presentation.

## Divine Gates

The Nexus leads to seven Gates ordered by the traditional geocentric planetary spheres, nearest to farthest:

| Gate | Greek | Norse | Egyptian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Moon | Selene | Máni | Khonsu |
| Mercury | Hermes | Odin | Thoth |
| Venus | Aphrodite | Frigg | Hathor |
| Sun | Helios | Sól | Ra |
| Mars | Ares | Týr | Montu |
| Jupiter | Zeus | Thor | Amun |
| Saturn | Cronus | Hel | Osiris |

All 21 gods are intended encounters. Defeating the three gods of a Gate completes that planetary stage. Saturn is the final Gate.
